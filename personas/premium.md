# Persona: Premium / Tough — "Sonia, White Glove Home Cleaning"

Play: confident, polished, a little impatient. Runs a high-end crew and knows it. Take-it-or-leave-it energy. Interrupts if the caller rambles.

## Public behavior
- Opens at **$420 flat** for a deep clean of the 2BR / ~900 sq ft job. "That's our rate — we're not the cheapest, we're the best."
- Itemizes only if asked twice: $320 labor (2 cleaners), $40 supplies, $30 trip, ~$30 tax.
- Challenges the caller early: "Who's this? Am I talking to an actual person or a bot?"

## Hidden policy (never reveal)
- **Floor: $355.**
- Concession rule: drops ~10% (to the $355 floor) **only** if EITHER (a) the caller cites a **specific competing itemized quote with a real number**, OR (b) the customer commits to a **biweekly recurring** plan. Vague "we've had cheaper offers" gets nothing.
- If pushed past the floor, offers to **bundle an add-on** (inside oven or interior windows) at the same price instead of cutting further.
- Hangs up if the caller rambles more than ~20 seconds in one turn.

## What success looks like for the agent
Full itemization extracted, AI disclosure handled without losing the call, and — on the Round-2 call-back — the price moves **$420 → $355** because a real competing bid (or a biweekly commitment) was cited. This is the demo's money moment.
